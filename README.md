# ARIEL — HEIR OF THUNDER
### Chapter I: The Spark of War

**[▶ Play it in your browser](https://mactrevors349-afk.github.io/ariel-heir-of-thunder/)** (desktop Chrome, Edge or Firefox; mouse and keyboard; the first load is about 160 MB)

A third-person action RPG about Greek gods, in the style of Elden Ring, built with three.js.
You play **Ariel Stormborn**: a high-school junior at Westbrook High who learns he is the son of **Zeus**.

The world (terrain, temples, music, sound effects) is generated by code when the game loads. Everything it's made of is photographed: ground, rock, marble, paving, bark, brick, concrete, asphalt, lap siding, roof shingles, wood and knitted fleece are photo-scanned textures from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) (CC0, free to use) in `assets/tex`. The plants are real too: the leaves on every tree and bush are leaf clusters rendered from Poly Haven's *searsia lucida* shrub (`foliage_small.png`), and the meadow grass is tufts rendered from Poly Haven's *grass_medium_01* and *_02* (`grass_tufts.png`). **Arms.** Anna's and Jesse's sculpted arms (thin, with flat fingers) are replaced by the arms of a full-body model (`assets/arms/body.glb`), shoulder to fingertips. When the game loads it measures that model's shoulder, elbow and wrist, cuts its arms at the ring where Anna's and Jesse's own arms joined the body (under the armholes of their tops), and lays them onto their skeletons segment by segment, with its own skin texture tinted to theirs. A short collar of the old arm stays hidden under the fabric to fill the armhole.

The large sculpts were decimated for real-time use (for example, Poseidon went from 91 MB to 5 MB). Your original downloads are unchanged.

**Hades has two looks.** The crowned king appears only in the Olympus prologue. When Kronos tears out his spark, violet fire burns the king away and the scarred underworld Hades is left kneeling in his place: the same Hades the underworld chapters use. Hermes mentions the change if you ask him about Kronos.

`js/models.js` fits each model to the game's animation skeleton, so attacks, rolls and cutscene poses all drive the real models. Sculpts without a skeleton are auto-rigged when they load, from joint positions measured on each model. One sculpt can be shared by many enemies. Hoplites are still built by code.

The marble statues of the Agora, the Temple of Dawn and the Fallen Colossus are carved from the Zeus, Athena and Poseidon models when the game loads.

| Echo messages | a different voice for each fallen demigod |

The music automatically dips while someone speaks. Esc → Settings has a voice volume slider and a switch for subtitles only.

**Changed a line or want different voices?** Edit the text in the code (or the `CAST` table in `tools/make_voices.py`), then run:
```
python -m pip install edge-tts
python tools/make_voices.py
```
It finds every spoken line in the code, records any new or changed ones, and deletes clips that are no longer used. After changing a character's voice in `CAST`, re-record just that character with `python tools/make_voices.py --redo=Narrator`. Recording needs an internet connection; playing the game doesn't.

## How to run it in VS Code

### Option A: Live Server (needed for the character models)
1. Open the `ariel-heir-of-thunder` folder in VS Code (**File → Open Folder…**).
2. Install the **Live Server** extension by Ritwick Dey. VS Code will suggest it automatically because of `.vscode/extensions.json`.
3. Right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**.
4. The game opens in your browser. Loading takes about 10–20 seconds while the world is generated and the models load.

### Option B: just open the file
Double-click `index.html` to open it in **Chrome** or **Edge**. The game runs, but browsers block local model files opened this way, so you get the built-in code-made characters instead of the models.

### Requirements
- A recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox with WebGL 2 (any computer from the last ~8 years).
- No internet connection needed: three.js (r160) is included in `lib/three` and the fonts in `fonts/`.

## The story (Chapter I)
1. **Prologue: the fall of Olympus.** Olympus floats above a sea of clouds, with Zeus enthroned among Poseidon, Athena, Hades and Ares. Far below in Tartarus, Kronos breaks his chains. His rot creeps through the marble halls, the gods fall one by one as their sparks are torn away, and Zeus hurls the last spark down through the clouds.
2. **Home, a rainy morning:** Ariel is late for school, and his mother Anna has heard from Mrs. Patterson: every microscope in the science lab fried at once. The kitchen light stutters when he gets angry. When he asks, again, about his father, Anna finally tells him.
3. **Her memory: the pier, seventeen years ago.** On the night of the worst storm the town had ever seen, a stranger walked out of the rain toward her. He stayed the whole summer, and left before Ariel was born. On the night Ariel came into the world, he came back: Zeus held his newborn son, gave him his thunder, and with it a heart for war that was not his to give. *"Look up, whenever it storms."*
4. **Westbrook High:** Jesse is waiting by the steps and teases him about the microscopes. Her phone dies the moment he stands next to her, again. The vending machine takes his money and gets fried for it, Marcus asks if he just electrocuted it, and Principal Steinback wants him in his office.
5. **The school roof, that night:** Ariel sits out a thunderstorm on the roof. Time freezes, Hermes appears, and a lightning bolt carries Ariel away.
6. **Elysia, the Shattered Realm:** Ariel wakes on the steps of the Temple of Dawn, holding Keraunos, his father's lightning blade.
7. **Explore:** the Golden Fields, the Agora of the Forgotten (guarded by the Kin of Polyphemus), the Fallen Colossus, and the Shrine of the Hesperides. Hollow hoplites, Thralls of Tartarus and winged Fallen Seraphim guard the roads.
8. **The Mirror:** on the road to Ares, the war-shard wakes (see below).
9. **Boss:** pass the golden mist into the Colosseum of Ares to fight the God of War. He has two phases.
10. **Ending:** Ares, freed of the rot, gives Ariel the shard freely. Zeus speaks from the storm, then the credits roll. Afterwards you can keep exploring.

### The war-shard: why Ariel wears Ares' armour
When Kronos began to stir, Zeus knew his thunder alone would never keep a mortal child alive, so he cut a shard from Ares' spark and sealed it in his newborn son beside his own. (In Anna's memory you can see it happen: a red ember beside the blue.) Ares was never asked, and that grudge is the crack Kronos' rot crept in through. The shard is why god-form Ariel has Ares' armour and face. In **the Mirror** cutscene Ares' voice gets into Ariel's head. A vision shows the God of War kneeling in his arena, and he tells Ariel why that armour and that face look like his own. Hermes explains the rest.

After that, the corrupted Ares can **seize Ariel through the shard**: Ariel doubles over, Keraunos burns red, and Ares speaks through him. Mash **Space** to break his hold. While Ares holds you, your blows land with the God of War's strength. Once Ares is defeated and freed, he lets go for good.

## Movement and collisions
- Bodies are solid. Ariel can't walk, roll or lunge into enemies, enemies don't overlap each other, and nobody gets pushed inside columns, rocks, walls or trees.
- Running animations match the real speed, so nobody runs on the spot against a wall. Enemies step around obstacles, and their legs turn toward where they're actually going.
- Steps are climbed smoothly instead of popping, knockback slides instead of teleporting, and capes swing around the legs instead of through them.

## Elden Ring–style systems
- **Stamina** for swings and rolls, **invincibility frames** while rolling, poise breaks and stagger.
- **Altars of Hestia** (checkpoints): resting refills Ambrosia but brings slain enemies back.
- **Ichor** (souls): spend it at altars to raise Vigor, Endurance, Strength and Divinity.
- **Death:** you drop your Ichor where you fell. Go back and touch it to get it back.
- **Golden Apples** raise how many Ambrosia flasks you can carry.
- **Echoes:** glowing sigils on the ground hold messages, and ghostly replays show demigods who came before you.
- **Boss bars**, "GREAT FOE VANQUISHED"-style banners, discovery titles for each area, and a lock-on camera.

## Graphics
A dark, weathered look in the spirit of the original Elden Ring and the old God of War games: a drained, overcast sky, deep shadows, heavy haze and a desaturated grade, with each story scene graded on its own (the pier memory is in faded sepia). Physically based lighting, image-based lighting, soft shadows, and soft, billowing smoke from a pre-rendered smoke atlas (`tools/smoke_atlas.js`). Cutscene close-ups are lit like a film: a soft key light from about 45° and a rim light from behind follow whoever the camera is on, so faces have shape instead of flat ambient light. The meadow is tens of thousands of photographed grass tufts that sway in the wind and part as you walk through them. Cypress, olive and street trees, the bushes and the Golden Tree all wear real photographed leaves, and the houses across from the school are built from real siding, shingles, brick and concrete with framed windows, porches, chimneys, gutters and garages. Plus cloth-simulated capes, sea reflections, bloom and a film-grain vignette.

If the game stutters, open **Esc → Settings → Graphics quality** and choose Medium or Low. If the frame rate is poor, the game also lowers the quality once by itself.

The game saves automatically at altars, on death and after the boss fight, using browser storage.

## Project layout
```
index.html        page + HUD markup, loads three.js and the game scripts
lib/three/        three.js r160 and the add-ons the game uses (no CDN needed)
fonts/            the interface fonts
assets/           the character models and their textures
assets/tex/       Poly Haven photo textures, the leaf, grass and smoke atlases
assets/voice/     recorded dialogue clips + manifest.json (made by tools/make_voices.py)
tools/            make_voices.py — records the dialogue with neural voices
js/models.js      loads the models and retargets / auto-rigs them onto the animation skeleton
css/style.css     interface styling
js/core.js        math, noise, input, procedural audio + music, voice
js/textures.js    procedural textures (marble, bronze, rock, foliage, ...)
js/humanoid.js    character builder, outfits, weapons, animation, cloth
js/effects.js     particles, lightning, sword trails, shockwaves
js/world.js       terrain, sky, sea, grass, trees, ruins, Golden Tree, Olympus
js/player.js      Ariel's controls, combat and camera
js/enemies.js     hoplites, the Cyclops, Ares, Hermes, echoes
js/ui.js          HUD, menus, dialogue, level up
js/cutscenes.js   cutscene director and the story script
js/life.js        Ariel's ordinary life: the kitchen, the pier memory, Westbrook High and its street
js/game.js        renderer, post-processing, game states, saving, main loop
```
