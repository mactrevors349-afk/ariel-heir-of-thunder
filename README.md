# ARIEL — HEIR OF THUNDER
### Chapter I: The Spark of War

**[▶ Play it in your browser](https://mactrevors349-afk.github.io/ariel-heir-of-thunder/)** (desktop Chrome, Edge or Firefox; mouse and keyboard; the first load is about 200 MB)

A third-person action RPG about Greek gods, in the style of Elden Ring, built with three.js.
You play **Ariel Stormborn**: a high-school junior at Westbrook High who learns he is the son of **Zeus**.

The world (terrain, temples, music) is generated by code when the game loads. Everything it's made of is photographed: ground, rock, marble, paving, bark, brick, concrete, asphalt, lap siding, roof shingles, wood and knitted fleece are photo-scanned textures from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) (CC0, free to use) in `assets/tex`. The plants are real too: the leaves on every tree and bush are leaf clusters rendered from Poly Haven's *searsia lucida* shrub (`foliage_small.png`), and the meadow grass is tufts rendered from Poly Haven's *grass_medium_01* and *_02* (`grass_tufts.png`). **Arms.** Anna's and Jesse's sculpted arms (thin, with flat fingers) are replaced by the arms of a full-body model (`assets/arms/body.glb`), shoulder to fingertips. When the game loads it measures that model's shoulder, elbow and wrist, cuts its arms at the ring where Anna's and Jesse's own arms joined the body (under the armholes of their tops), and lays them onto their skeletons segment by segment, with its own skin texture tinted to theirs. A short collar of the old arm stays hidden under the fabric to fill the armhole. The arms' skin is toned to match her neck and face under the game's lights. Where the sleeve covers the arm, and where her shoulders lie under the top, the skin is painted in the top's own cloth, so the join between sleeve and armhole never shows a slit of bare shoulder.

**Hair and mouths.** Anna's and Jesse's sculpted hair is shaded as hair: fine strands running down from the crown, each a little lighter or darker than the next, catching the light across their grain. Their mouths are carved shut, so each is found on the face itself (the line between the lips, below the tip of the nose), and the jaw below it swings open when they speak, with the dark of the mouth between the parted lips.

The large sculpts were decimated for real-time use (for example, Poseidon went from 91 MB to 5 MB). Your original downloads are unchanged.

**Hades has two looks.** The crowned king appears only in the Olympus prologue. When Kronos tears out his spark, violet fire burns the king away and the scarred underworld Hades is left kneeling in his place: the same Hades the underworld chapters use. Hermes mentions the change if you ask him about Kronos.

`js/models.js` fits each model to the game's animation skeleton, so attacks, rolls and cutscene poses all drive the real models. Sculpts without a skeleton are auto-rigged when they load, from joint positions measured on each model. One sculpt can be shared by many enemies. Hoplites are still built by code.

The marble statues of the Agora, the Temple of Dawn and the Fallen Colossus are carved from the Zeus, Athena and Poseidon models when the game loads.

### Voices
Every line of dialogue is voiced with Microsoft neural voices, stored as MP3s in `assets/voice`. Each character has a natural voice that matches their age and gender, recorded at its own pitch: the recording service's pitch shifting made voices sound processed, so the gods' depth now comes from the game playing them back a touch slower with a low-end lift. Every line gets its own delivery on top of that: shouted lines come quicker, higher and louder, lines that trail off (…) slower, questions lift at the end, and short lines are slowed so they aren't clipped.

### Acting
Nobody stands still while they talk. Whoever is speaking performs the line, and everyone else on screen turns to listen (`Perform`, `Face` and the speaking layer of `Rig`):

- **Lips.** Each line's mouth shapes are worked out from its words and stretched over the recording, then gated by the recording's loudness, so a mouth never moves in a silence.
  - Teenage Ariel's model carries the full set of mouth shapes, so he is properly lip-synced.
  - Zeus opens his jaw bone, and Marcus his one open-mouth shape.
  - Anna and Jesse open their sculpted jaws (see *Hair and mouths* above).
  - God-form Ariel and Ares speak behind their helms; Hermes' face stays still.
- **Faces.** Ariel blinks, his brows and mouth take the mood of the line (a question lifts them, anger sets them, a line that trails off softens them), and his eyes find whoever he is talking to.
- **Heads and hands.** The speaker's head nods on the stressed syllables over a slow, wandering tilt. Their free hand rises through a phrase, beats on its stresses and settles in its pauses. A hand holding a mug, a phone, a sword or a staff stays put.
- **Listening.** Everyone else turns their head, and a little of their shoulders, toward the speaker. Jesse keeps her eyes on her phone while she is texting.
- A voice-over (Anna telling her memory over the pier, Jesse's flashbacks) moves nobody on screen.

### Camera
- A held shot is never quite still: it creeps slowly in on its subject, most in a close shot and hardly at all in a wide one.
- Conversations are covered the way films cover them: past one person's shoulder onto the other's face with a long lens, then a single on the one who answers, closer for the lines that matter.
  - The over-the-shoulder shot works out its own framing (`Cutscene.over`). The near head always sits at the edge of the frame, whatever the lens or the shape of the screen.
  - When the two stand far apart (Hermes across the roof or the meadow), the camera stands further back on a longer lens, so the far one is still seen from the waist up.
  - It never backs into a wall or a column behind the near one.
  - Over god-form Ariel it sits further out, clear of his helm and the great plates on his shoulders.
- In the kitchen Ariel now comes right up to his mother instead of talking across the room.
- Hermes is filmed from the waist up on the roof, in Elysia and at the Mirror, not as a small figure in the distance.
  - In Elysia he turns to face Ariel while they talk, and turns to the Colosseum only when he points the way.
  - On the roof his glow is soft enough that the folds of his tunic still show.
- Ares' speech after the fight cuts to Ariel hearing it, from the kneeling god's own eye line, looking up at the boy who beat him. It then closes in for Ares' last line.


**Changed a line or want different voices?** Edit the text in the code (or the `CAST` table in `tools/make_voices.py`), then run:
```
python -m pip install edge-tts
python tools/make_voices.py
```
It finds every spoken line in the code, records any new or changed ones, and deletes clips that are no longer used. After changing a character's voice in `CAST`, re-record just that character with `python tools/make_voices.py --redo=Narrator`. Recording needs an internet connection; playing the game doesn't.

## How to run it in VS Code

### Option A: Live Server (needed for the character models)
1. Open the `ariel-heir-of-thunder` folder in VS Code (**File → Open Folder…**).
2. Install the **Live Server** extension by Ritwick Dey. VS Code will suggest it automatically because of `.vscode/extensions.json`.
3. Right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**.
4. The game opens in your browser. Loading takes about 10–20 seconds while the world is generated and the models load.

### Option B: just open the file
Double-click `index.html` to open it in **Chrome** or **Edge**. The game runs, but browsers block local model files opened this way, so you get the built-in code-made characters instead of the models.

### Requirements
- A recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox with WebGL 2 (any computer from the last ~8 years).
- No internet connection needed: three.js (r160) is included in `lib/three` and the fonts in `fonts/`.

## The story (Chapter I)
1. **Prologue: the fall of Olympus.** Olympus floats above a sea of clouds, with Zeus enthroned among Poseidon, Athena, Hades and Ares. Far below in Tartarus, Kronos breaks his chains. His rot creeps through the marble halls, the gods fall one by one as their sparks are torn away, and Zeus hurls the last spark down through the clouds.
2. **Home, a rainy morning:** Ariel is late for school, and his mother Anna has heard from Mrs. Patterson: every microscope in the science lab fried at once. The kitchen light stutters when he gets angry. When he asks, again, about his father, Anna finally tells him.
3. **Her memory: the pier, seventeen years ago.** On the night of the worst storm the town had ever seen, a stranger walked out of the rain toward her. He stayed the whole summer, and left before Ariel was born. On the night Ariel came into the world, he came back: Zeus held his newborn son, gave him his thunder, and with it a heart for war that was not his to give. *"Look up, whenever it storms."*
4. **Westbrook High:** Jesse is waiting by the steps and teases him about the microscopes. Her phone dies the moment he stands next to her, again. The vending machine takes his money and gets fried for it, Marcus asks if he just electrocuted it, and Principal Steinback wants him in his office.
5. **The school roof, that night:** Ariel sits out a thunderstorm on the roof. Time freezes, Hermes appears, and a lightning bolt carries Ariel away.
6. **Elysia, the Shattered Realm:** Ariel wakes on the steps of the Temple of Dawn, holding Keraunos, his father's lightning blade.
7. **Explore:** the Golden Fields, the Agora of the Forgotten (guarded by the Kin of Polyphemus), the Fallen Colossus, and the Shrine of the Hesperides. Hollow hoplites (dead Spartans still under arms: crested bronze helmets, greaves, round shields, skin drained grey, a faint smoke coming off them), Thralls of Tartarus and winged Fallen Seraphim guard the roads.
8. **The Mirror:** on the road to Ares, the war-shard wakes (see below).
9. **Boss:** pass the golden mist into the Colosseum of Ares to fight the God of War. He has two phases.
10. **Ending:** the rot burns out of the beaten Ares, and for a moment he seems himself again. Then he stands. The rot never made him, he says: he opened the gates of Olympus to Kronos himself, and the storms that hunted Anna's house for seventeen years were his. He reaches into Ariel through the shard and forces him to his knees. Then:
    - **The Berserker:** Ariel's heart stops, and his eyes ignite in the helm. Lightning comes down on him and fire comes up out of the ground. He rises with Ares' war-red burning through his armour and Zeus' lightning crawling over it, and speaks with a voice that echoes. Ares charges and Ariel takes him apart with Thunder Steps, each a flash of lightning. A last cut throws Ares on his back, and Ariel leaps into the storm, hangs there while the sky pours into his blade, and comes down through Ares' chest.
    - **Stone and ash:** stone spreads out from the wound while Ares says his last words. Ariel pulls the blade from stone, and the God of War crumbles into ash on the wind.
    - **The new God of War:** Ariel tears Ares' spark out of the air and takes it into himself. Thunder and fire crown him, and the title card reads *ARIEL — God of War*. Zeus speaks from the storm, mourning both his sons, and warns Ariel to learn when to set the war down.
    - **The Fates' tapestry:** narrated slides painted as a Greek black-figure vase. They are drawn live from the game's own 3D figures (glaze silhouettes, incised detail, meander, rays, Greek name labels and rosettes) and show the war for the sparks, Kronos cast down, the gods home again and Olympus shining.
    - **Olympus restored (3D):** the rot drains out of the hall, each god's spark comes home, and Ariel stands at Zeus's right hand.
    - **Epilogue, *The Two Lives of Ariel Stormborn*:**
      - Ariel comes home to Anna and chooses to stay her son.
      - Jesse works out the truth on the school steps. As she lists what gave him away, the game cuts to flashbacks of those moments in a cold, faded memory grade: her phone dying in her hands, the vending machine frying, and the lightning on the school roof.
      - Painted scenes of his mortal life (school by day, god by night, graduation, and the wedding, where Zeus comes as a summer rain and one place at the table is set for the brother whose war Ariel carries).
      - The **wedding photograph** on the pier where Anna met Zeus: Ariel in a tailcoat, Jesse in an ivory gown with veil and bouquet.
      - Years later, on **Olympus**, their son **Kratos** is born. Jesse wears a goddess gown, Anna is in white and gold, Ariel is in his god form, and Zeus looks on from his throne.
      - Then **Deimos**, the brothers growing up between Westbrook and Olympus, and the brothers as the myths remember them.
        - **Kratos** grows into his name, Strength. He stands at Zeus' right hand with his sister Bia, Force. He holds the Titan Prometheus to the Caucasus rock while Hephaestus drives the chains, and later walks beside Heracles on the day the chains are broken.
        - **Deimos**, Dread, rides out at the side of his father, the new War-god, and armies lay down their spears before a blow is struck.
        - Four of these slides cut away to short 3D scenes and back: Deimos grown, before a crag under a blood-red sky; Kratos beside Zeus' throne, then hauling the Titan's chain on the storm-lit Caucasus while the shackles ring; at dawn Heracles breaks the chain with his hands as Kratos looks on; and Deimos walking out with his father as a line of hoplites goes down on one knee. Kratos wears his father's god-armour worked in dark bronze, with a crimson cloak.
      - Then Anna grows old, and the credits roll.

    (Kratos, "strength", and Deimos, "dread", are figures from Greek myth: Kratos and Bia at Zeus' throne in Hesiod's *Theogony*, Kratos binding Prometheus in Aeschylus' *Prometheus Bound*, and Deimos beside Ares in Hesiod and the *Iliad*. They are written fresh for this story.) Afterwards you can keep exploring.

### The war-shard: why Ariel wears Ares' armour
When Kronos began to stir, Zeus knew his thunder alone would never keep a mortal child alive, so he cut a shard from Ares' spark and sealed it in his newborn son beside his own. (In Anna's memory you can see it happen: a red ember beside the blue.) Ares was never asked, and that grudge is the crack Kronos' rot crept in through. The shard is why god-form Ariel has Ares' armour and face. In **the Mirror** cutscene Ares' voice gets into Ariel's head. A vision shows the God of War kneeling in his arena, and he tells Ariel why that armour and that face look like his own. Hermes explains the rest.

After that, the corrupted Ares can **seize Ariel through the shard**: Ariel doubles over, Keraunos burns red, and Ares speaks through him. Mash **Space** to break his hold. While Ares holds you, your blows land with the God of War's strength. Ares tries it once more after the fight, and that is what wakes the Berserker.

## Ariel's powers
Ariel is a demigod, and plays like one. Keraunos hits hard, his armour turns a fifth of every blow, his health slowly comes back by itself, and it takes a heavy blow to knock him down.

Each of the six gifts on keys **1–6** has its own cooldown (shown on its slot) and costs FP or stamina. Every third cut of a combo lets the lightning stored in Keraunos go: it arcs on to the next foes.

**Rage** (the bar under stamina) fills as you deal and take blows. When it is full, press **G** for **Berserker God Mode**: the fight holds its breath, and Zeus and Ares tower out of the sky behind Ariel (Zeus in his storm, Ares in his fire) and pour their power into him. He rises, and the shockwave throws back everything near him. The first time plays in full; after that it is a few beats and a line. For 25 seconds:
- you deal double damage and take about a third
- nothing staggers you, and stamina is unlimited
- FP pours back in and the gifts recharge three times as fast
- flying is free
- every cut carries lightning and fire with it
- Ariel's armour burns at the edges

## How the fighting feels
Every blow is built to be felt, not just counted.
- **Weight on every hit.** When Keraunos connects, the game freezes for a split second (longer for the third cut and the slam, longer still for a big foe), and the camera is knocked along the blow and springs back. Light cuts give a small knock, the slam a heavy thud. The shake keeps going through the freeze, and the "Camera shake" setting turns all of it off.
- **The last foe.** Killing the last enemy nearby (or any giant) slows time for a moment, so you see him go down.
- **Cuts that land where the blade is.** The two opening cuts were rebuilt so the sword really sweeps through the enemy at chest height, and the hit counts at that moment, not during the wind-up. Each cut turns into its target over the wind-up and steps in just far enough to reach it, no further.
- **Enemies react to where they were hit.** A cut from the left twists them right, a blow from behind throws them forward. Light enemies flinch out of their own wind-up if you hit them first, so a combo isn't traded blow for blow. They can't be held forever, though: after a few flinches they swing back through it.
- **They fall the way the blow drove them**: thrown onto their back from the front (arms flung, a stagger, a slam into the ground), onto their face from behind, sliding a little with it. Before, a slain hoplite stood frozen until it faded.
- **You can see an attack coming.** A glint flashes on an enemy's weapon just before it swings and follows the blade as it draws back: white for a normal blow, red for one that will knock you down.
- **Nothing you press is lost.** A press made during a swing, a roll or a stagger is remembered for half a second and happens at the first moment it can: the next cut, a roll out of a swing, a cut straight out of a roll. Moving cuts the end of a swing or roll short.
- **Perfect dodge.** Roll at the last instant before a blow lands and time slows, stamina comes back, and your next cut is a counter: it dashes to the enemy that missed, hits harder and breaks its guard.
- **Getting hit.** Ariel is thrown back from a blow in front and stumbles forward from one behind, and the camera jolts with it.
- **The camera keeps the fight in view.** In a fight, when you leave the mouse alone, it turns a little so the nearest enemy stands to Ariel's right instead of hidden behind his back, keeps up with an enemy circling him, and leans toward a giant so its head stays in the picture. Move the mouse and it's yours again.
- **Effects that don't blind you.** Sword trails ride the outer part of the blade and fade even during the freeze, and the thunder slam's flash no longer whites out the screen.
- **The cape behaves like cloth.** It can't stretch into a board during a roll, doesn't flip over his head when a swing stops, and settles after a shove instead of floating.

## Movement and collisions
- Bodies are solid. Ariel can't walk, roll or lunge into enemies, enemies don't overlap each other, and nobody gets pushed inside columns, rocks, walls or trees.
- Running animations match the real speed, so nobody runs on the spot against a wall. Enemies step around obstacles, and their legs turn toward where they're actually going.
- Steps are climbed smoothly instead of popping, knockback slides instead of teleporting, and capes swing around the legs instead of through them.

## Elden Ring–style systems
- **Stamina** for swings and rolls, **invincibility frames** while rolling, poise breaks and stagger.
- **Altars of Hestia** (checkpoints): resting refills Ambrosia but brings slain enemies back.
- **Ichor** (souls): spend it at altars to raise Vigor, Endurance, Strength and Divinity.
- **Death:** you drop your Ichor where you fell. Go back and touch it to get it back.
- **Golden Apples** raise how many Ambrosia flasks you can carry.
- **Echoes:** glowing sigils on the ground hold messages, and ghostly replays show demigods who came before you.
- **Boss bars**, "GREAT FOE VANQUISHED"-style banners, discovery titles for each area, and a lock-on camera.

## HUD
Laid out like a modern action MMO:
- **Top left:** quest cards (the main quest, the Altars of Hestia and the golden apples, each with its progress) and a log under them that records areas entered, foes slain and items found.
- **Top centre:** a system line for notices.
- **Top right:** a square, north-up map painted from the terrain, with foes in red, the altars, the apples, Hermes, and your view cone.
- **Over foes:** nameplates with health and a thin poise line.
- **Under Ariel:** the distance to the current objective, with an arrow toward it.
- **Bottom centre:** a slim slot bar. Health and Favour sit over it. Zeus's Bolt and gifts are on the left, Ares's gifts and flight on the right, and God Mode is in the middle inside the Rage ring. Ambrosia is the diamond with the count under it.
- **Bottom left:** name, level and Ichor, with a gold line along the bottom edge showing Ichor toward the next level.

The whole HUD scales with the window.

## Graphics
A late-afternoon look in the spirit of God of War:
- a low golden sun throwing long shadows across the meadow, filled by a cool sky light
- a hazy gold sky toward the sun, with blue still in it above and behind
- distant hills and mountains fading into a pale blue haze behind the warm foreground
- a filmic grade with fuller colour

Each story scene is still graded on its own (the pier memory is in faded sepia). The boulders are smooth, weathered stone, and the Fallen Colossus lies among broken marble blocks. Physically based lighting, image-based lighting, soft shadows, and soft, billowing smoke from a pre-rendered smoke atlas (`tools/smoke_atlas.js`). Shadows in the kitchen, on the pier and in the school yard are drawn finer and softer, so the shadow of a nose or an arm no longer falls in visible squares in close-up. Cutscene close-ups are lit like a film: a soft key light from about 45° and a rim light from behind follow whoever the camera is on, so faces have shape instead of flat ambient light. The meadow is tens of thousands of photographed grass tufts that sway in the wind and part as you walk through them. Cypress, olive and street trees, the bushes and the Golden Tree all wear real photographed leaves, and the houses across from the school are built from real siding, shingles, brick and concrete with framed windows, porches, chimneys, gutters and garages. Plus cloth-simulated capes, sea reflections, bloom and a film-grain vignette.

**Powers.** Every power and every scene where a god uses one share the same effects:
- **Lightning:** camera-facing ribbons with a thin, blinding white core inside a soft blue corona. Each bolt forks, and its forks fork again. The leader races down the channel, the bolt flashes, re-strikes two or three times, then fades. A god's thunderbolt in a cutscene keeps burning and flickering.
- **Sparks and embers:** streaks stretched along their flight that cool from white-hot to dull red, and skip off the ground.
- **Fire:** soft wisps eaten into licking tongues by noise that rises with them, burning yellow in the thick of it, orange, then deep red at the edges, with dark smoke rolling up after.
- **Shock waves:** a faint pressure ring with dust kicked up along it.
- **The Spear of Ares:** a dark bronze spear with an orange-hot head, trailing an unbroken stream of fire.
- **The gods' sparks:** orbs that are blinding at the heart and fade at the rim, with arcs leaping off them.
- **God Mode:** arcs of electricity crawl over Ariel's armour.

**Blood.** In the God of War style: every blow throws a spurt of dark blood along the cut, a rope of drops that arcs out over a tenth of a second, with a fan of fine spray, a few heavy drops and a dark mist. Each drop is drawn stretched along its flight and shaded like a bead of liquid with a wet highlight. The drops land as glossy splatters with satellite drops flung the way the blow went. A pool spreads under each body that falls. Each kind bleeds its own colour: red for the living and for Ares, dark old blood for the Hollow, black-purple for the Thralls of Tartarus. Burns and lightning bleed far less, because they sear the wound.

Three effects are worked out from the depth the scene has already drawn, so they cost no second render of the world (`js/ao.js`):
- **Ambient occlusion** gives every crease, corner, grass root and body on the ground its soft contact shadow.
- **Sun shafts:** when you face the low sun, its light streams round whatever stands against the sky, such as temple columns, trees and Ariel himself.
- **Depth of field:** in cutscene close-ups the background softens behind whoever the camera is on, like a film lens.

All three are on from the Medium preset up.

If the game stutters, open **Esc → Settings → Graphics quality** and choose Medium or Low. If the frame rate is poor, the game also lowers the quality once by itself.

The game saves automatically at altars, on death and after the boss fight, using browser storage.

## Project layout
```
index.html        page + HUD markup, loads three.js and the game scripts
lib/three/        three.js r160 and the add-ons the game uses (no CDN needed)
fonts/            the interface fonts
assets/           the character models and their textures
assets/tex/       Poly Haven photo textures, the leaf, grass and smoke atlases
assets/voice/     recorded dialogue clips + manifest.json (made by tools/make_voices.py)
assets/sfx/       recorded hits, footsteps and cloth (Kenney, CC0), layered over the synthesized sounds
tools/            make_voices.py — records the dialogue with neural voices
js/models.js      loads the models and retargets / auto-rigs them onto the animation skeleton
css/style.css     interface styling
js/core.js        math, noise, input, procedural audio + music, voice
js/textures.js    procedural textures (marble, bronze, rock, foliage, ...)
js/humanoid.js    character builder, outfits, weapons, animation, cloth
js/effects.js     particles, lightning, sword trails, shockwaves
js/world.js       terrain, sky, sea, grass, trees, ruins, Golden Tree, Olympus
js/player.js      Ariel's controls, combat and camera
js/enemies.js     hoplites, the Cyclops, Ares, Hermes, echoes
js/ui.js          HUD, menus, dialogue, level up
js/cutscenes.js   cutscene director and the story script
js/life.js        Ariel's ordinary life: the kitchen, the pier memory, Westbrook High and its street
js/ao.js          ambient occlusion, sun shafts and cutscene depth of field (from the scene's depth)
js/game.js        renderer, post-processing, game states, saving, main loop
```

### Developer shortcuts
Add these to the URL to jump straight in:
- `?skip`: skip the intro and start at the Temple of Dawn
- `?boss`: start at the gate of the Colosseum of Ares
- `?rage`: start with a full Rage bar, to try God Mode straight away
- `?god`: Ariel can't be hurt (for testing)
